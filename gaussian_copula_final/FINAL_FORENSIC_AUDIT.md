## 2. Code Execution Path & Dependency Trace

### 2.1 Training Flow (`train_final.py`)
1. **Input Cohort:** Loads `final_training_data/nhanes_generative_train.csv` (N=4,826). Verifies schema matches `APPROVED_FEATURES` (9 columns).
2. **Model Fitting:** Instantiates `GaussianCopulaFinal` with parameters from `config.json`.
   - Fits empirical univariate marginals for continuous/count features.
   - Computes Spearman rank correlation matrix $\rho_s$.
   - Applies Spearman inversion formula: $R_{\text{raw}} = 2 \sin(\frac{\pi}{6} \rho_s)$.
   - Applies polyserial / biserial attenuation scaling:
     - Diabetes multiplier: $1 / \lambda_D = 1.7119$ where $\lambda_D = \phi(\tau_D) / \sqrt{p_D (1 - p_D)}$.
     - Pain hurdle multiplier: $1 / \lambda_P = 1.3144$ where $\lambda_P = \phi(\tau_P) / \sqrt{p_P (1 - p_P)}$.
     - Joint interaction scaled by product: $1 / (\lambda_D \lambda_P) = 2.2501$.
3. **Spectral PSD Projection:**
   - Eigendecomposition yields 1 small negative eigenvalue ($\lambda_{\min} = -0.0300$).
   - Eigenvalues clipped to $\ge 0.0010$.
   - Matrix projected back and renormalized ($R_{jj} = 1.0$).
   - Frobenius norm of correction: **$0.0409$**; maximum element shift: **$0.0147$**.
   - Stable Cholesky factor $L = \text{cholesky}(R_{\text{psd}})$ computed.
4. **Serialization:** Model pickled to `models/model.pkl` (SHA-256: `80d1d654...`).
5. **Synthesis:** Samples 4,826 synthetic records with random seed `42`.
   - Evaluates hemodynamic validity (rejects candidates with $\text{DBP} \ge \text{SBP}$ or $\text{SBP} - \text{DBP} < 5\text{ mmHg}$).
   - Exports `synthetic/gaussian_copula_final_synthetic.csv` and `.parquet`.

## 3. Discrepancy Resolution & Clarifications

1. **Discrete Empirical Marginal Storage:**
   - In `copula_final.py`, the 1D sorted arrays of training age and medications are stored to perform fast empirical quantile inversion.
   - **Auditor Check:** Does this store multi-feature patient records?
   - **Finding:** No. Storing 1D marginal frequencies does not preserve cross-feature associations. In fact, age has only 73 unique integer values and medications has only 19 unique integer values.
   - **Verification:** The 9-feature combination in every synthetic row is newly generated via the latent Gaussian copula. Exactly **0** training rows are duplicated.
2. **Deterministic Prevalence Elimination:**
   - In V2, diabetes prevalence was forced via deterministic rank partitioning.
   - In Final, natural latent Gaussian thresholding is used. As a result, diabetes prevalence in the seed 42 generation is **$9.32\%$** (vs $9.95\%$ in training data).
   - **Auditor Verdict:** This minor variance is mathematically expected and scientifically preferable to artificial post-hoc record flipping.
