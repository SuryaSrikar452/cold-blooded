# FINAL_MODEL_CARD.md: Model Card for Gaussian Copula Final

Following standard Model Cards for Model Reporting (Mitchell et al., 2019).

---

## 1. Model Details

- **Model Name:** `GaussianCopulaFinal` (Version: `final_hardened`)
- **Model Type:** Hybrid Gaussian-Copula-based synthetic tabular data generator with variable-specific marginal handling, latent dependence modeling, hurdle treatment for zero-inflated pain, and latent threshold modeling for diabetes.
- **Developers:** SH-405 Senior ML Engineering Team
- **Release Date:** September 2026
- **License:** Open Research Benchmark License
- **Frameworks:** Python 3.14.0, SciPy 1.17.0, NumPy 2.4.1, Pandas 2.3.3, Scikit-learn 1.8.0.

---

## 2. Intended Use

### Primary Intended Uses
- Generation of realistic, clinically plausible synthetic patient cohorts for developing and testing healthcare machine learning pipelines without accessing Protected Health Information (PHI).
- Benchmarking clinical trial cohort selection algorithms and feasibility queries.
- Providing educational and hackathon training cohorts with intact multivariate dependencies.

### Out-of-Scope / Prohibited Uses
- **Clinical Decision Support:** Must NOT be used to guide individual patient diagnosis, pharmacological prescription, or therapeutic intervention.
- **Causal Claims:** Must NOT be interpreted as establishing biological or pharmacological causation (observational correlation only).
- **Longitudinal Ground Truth:** Multi-visit trajectories generated by downstream modules are synthetic persistence simulations and must NOT be cited as empirical longitudinal disease progression.

---

## 3. Factors & Demographic Subgroups

The model conditions on demographic factors available in NHANES:
- **`age`**: Bounded between $8$ and $80$ years (integer).
- **`sex`**: Biological sex ($1 = \text{Male}, 2 = \text{Female}$).
- **`diabetes`**: Diagnostic status ($0 = \text{Non-diabetic}, 1 = \text{Diagnosed}$).

### Subgroup Performance Observations
- Diabetic subgroups exhibit higher mean systolic blood pressure ($127.8\text{ mmHg}$ vs $116.8\text{ mmHg}$ in non-diabetics) and higher prescription medication counts ($4.8$ vs $1.3$), closely matching real clinical epidemiology.
- Pediatric cohorts ($8 \le \text{age} < 18$) reflect non-diabetic status and zero-to-low medication counts, adhering to clinical realities.

---

## 4. Training Data

- **Source Dataset:** CDC National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) continuous survey cycles.
- **Training Population:** $N = 4,826$ participants (`final_training_data/nhanes_generative_train.csv`).
- **Feature Set:** Exactly 9 features: `age`, `sex`, `systolic_bp`, `diastolic_bp`, `activity_mims`, `n_medications`, `adherence_pct`, `pain_score`, `diabetes`.
- **Benchmark-Augmented Variables:** `adherence_pct` and `pain_score` are mathematically synthesized benchmark variables and not native CDC NHANES survey laboratory measurements.
- **Data Preprocessing:** Standardized curation with missing-value imputation and outlier verification performed in audited upstream project stages.

---

## 5. Evaluation Data & Methodology

- **Holdout Set:** $N = 1,207$ participants (`final_training_data/nhanes_real_holdout.csv`).
- **Zero Leakage Guarantee:** The holdout was **never** used during model fitting, distribution selection, threshold tuning, or calibration. Evaluated strictly once on the finalized, frozen model.
- **Evaluation Dimensions:**
  - Marginal fidelity (KS statistic, Wasserstein distance)
  - Multivariate dependence (Pearson and Spearman correlation drift, 2D JSD)
  - Clinical validity (hemodynamic consistency, non-negativity)
  - Discriminator distinguishability (5-fold cross-validated logistic regression)
  - Downstream utility (TSTR ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, F1 score)
  - Privacy diagnostics (exact matches, size-matched nearest-neighbor distance)

## 7. Ethical Considerations & Caveats

- **No Differential Privacy Guarantee:** The generator relies on parametric and empirical copula transformations. While empirical memorization tests demonstrate zero exact matches and healthy nearest-neighbor distances, this does **not** provide formal mathematical $(\epsilon, \delta)$-Differential Privacy guarantees.
- **No Claims of Perfection:** The model captures linear and monotonic rank dependencies via a Gaussian copula. Non-linear, higher-order tail dependencies may be smoothed.
- **Transparent Terminology:** The synthetic data is described as exhibiting *reduced distinguishability* under linear classification, but is not claimed to be "indistinguishable" or "identical" to real clinical records.
