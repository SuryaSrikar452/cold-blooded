02 / Causal structure

Not random data. It's learned.

Synthetic health records aren't built through independent coin flips. In human biology, blood pressure relates to age and weight, activity relates to pain, and adherence to medication shapes how symptoms resolve. Synthia preserves those relationships so synthetic patients behave like real ones — statistically, not individually.
